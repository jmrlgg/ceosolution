## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and improve your experience.

## We use cookies to:

- Enhance website performance and functionality.
- Provide a personalized experience based on your preferences.
- Analyze website traffic and usage patterns to improve our services.

## Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential Cookies:** These are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be turned off. They are used to provide essential services like secure login or cart functionality.
- **Performance Cookies:** These cookies collect data on how visitors use our website (e.g., most visited pages) to improve performance and user experience.
- **Functional Cookies:** These cookies allow us to remember your preferences (like language or region) to provide a more personalized experience.
- **Advertising and Tracking Cookies:** These cookies are used to provide tailored ads based on your browsing history and preferences.

## Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, but this may affect your experience on our site. For more information on how to manage cookies, refer to your browser’s help section.
